# Superfund Sites

This project was made to show every area within the United States that is designated as a superfund sites. The sites are classified based on their active status established by the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). The EPA also has established regions within the United States. As such any issues regarding superfund sites can be directed towards these regional offices. Information regarding a superfund site can be accessed just by hovering the mouse over a site.

## Packages
- Leaflet.js 
- jQuery 
- Leaflet.css
- FontAwesome 
- Chroma.js 

## Data
All data was provided by the Environmental Protection Agency @ EPA and hosted by the Department of Homeland Security.
